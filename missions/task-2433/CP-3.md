- SC7 (happy path): 200 + `completed`, exactly one dispatch, one SSE progress event carrying the dispatched operation ID.
- SC8 (no bypass): scan of `src/interfaces/web/**` finds no `child_process`, `exec`, `spawn`, or shell-string/`px`-invocation path (matches are the pre-existing `px <command>` display text in the snapshot contract and `execution` error-kind literals).
