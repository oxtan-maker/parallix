## Lifecycle and real-agent end-to-end audit

`test/e2e-mission-lifecycle.test.ts` (10 cases) and `test/e2e-real-agent-smoke.test.ts` (one scenario driver
per configured agent/runner pair) were compared helper by helper. They stay separate and unchanged:

- The lifecycle suite runs the production CLI in process against a fake-agent preload and its
  `runCommand` asserts that no `codex` or `opencode` executable is launched. The real-agent suite
  launches the configured runner against a reachable model backend, holds the custom-model lock, runs
  health checks, and classifies agent failures. A shared helper would have to drop one of those guards.
- Of the four helpers whose names coincide (`runCommand`, `runGit`, `maybeCommandPath`, `shouldKeepTmp`),
  only `shouldKeepTmp` (four lines) is identical. `runCommand` differs by the mocked-agent guard,
  `runGit` by option pass-through, and `maybeCommandPath` by a login-shell probe fallback, so they are
  not semantically equivalent and are not merged.
- Both stay in `AGENT_E2E_TESTS` in `test/lib/test-categories.ts`, outside the default and integration
  lanes, with their own commands; both remain on the documented file-size debt list in
  `test/file-size-cap.test.ts`, so their size does not grow.
- No case, watchdog, cleanup path, or tier changes. No scenario is removed.
