## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Spin out the focused-specialist-agent alternative from context-management research mission px-0008 into a separate research mission. Evaluate whether bounded specialist exploration and concise handoffs reduce total mission effort while preserving delivery quality. This is a coordination and context experiment, large enough to own its own protocol, replay harness and evidence.

Scope: compare a single implementer with a coordinator delegating bounded exploration to focused specialists on identical frozen mission inputs. Match model and settings initially; separate role specialization from changing models or adding compute. Measure aggregate coordinator, specialist and reviewer tokens, elapsed time, duplicated work, handoff omissions, validation and independent-review outcomes. Preserve native sessions and repositories.

Research only: no production delegation policy, workflow/lifecycle changes, global model defaults or automatic rollout. Any implementation supported by the results requires a separate mission. Rebase-conflict context experiments remain in px-0008 and are not a dependency of this mission.
<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:END -->

## Acceptance Criteria
<!-- AC:BEGIN -->
- [ ] #1 Freeze hypotheses, sample selection, treatments, aggregate accounting, common recovery allowances and finite budgets before model calls; separate concurrency and model changes from specialist-context effects.
