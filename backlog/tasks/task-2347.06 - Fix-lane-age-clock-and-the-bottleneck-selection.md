## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
Two defects make the board's lane age and its bottleneck sentence unreliable.

**Age is measured against the last telemetry event, not now.**
`ConcreteMetricsReadAdapter` passes `asOf: instants.at(-1)`
(`src/application/projections/metrics-read-adapter.ts:74`), where `instants` are
derived from recorded event timestamps
(`:134-159`). `medianAgeByLaneSeries`
(`src/application/projections/metrics.ts:258`) then ages every mission against
that value. If the last transition happened Friday and the operator opens the
board on Monday, a mission stalled for three days is still reported as minutes
old — exactly the situation the metric is supposed to expose. Missions with no
transition at all are absent from the calculation entirely.

`instants` also mixes hour-truncated transition stamps with usage `date` values
turned into midnight (`:143`, `:149`), so `asOf` can land *before* the newest
transition; the resulting negative ages are silently dropped by the
`minutes >= 0` guard, removing whole lanes from the series without any signal.

**The bottleneck is chosen over terminal lanes.** `bottleneckNarrative`
(`src/application/projections/metrics.ts:281`) picks the maximum median age over
`BOARD_LANES`, which includes `integration` and `done`
(`:37`). Missions that closed months ago keep accumulating age in `done`, so the
"oldest lane" is almost always `done` once any history exists, and the sentence
shown to the operator is structurally uninformative.

### Required fix

Age current lanes against the projection time supplied by an injected clock (so
tests stay deterministic), give missions without a recorded transition an
entered-at timestamp from the lifecycle history added in task-2347.02, and restrict
the bottleneck to lanes where work can actually wait, excluding terminal lanes
and completed missions. Report explicitly when a lane has no measurable age
rather than dropping it.
